# Export Policy

Default downstream sales export surface:
- `seller_ready_leads.csv/jsonl`

Lane-specific seller exports:
- `seller_ready_bid_now_leads.csv/jsonl`
- `seller_ready_pre_position_leads.csv/jsonl`

Non-seller exports kept for research and audit:
- `research_ready_leads.csv/jsonl`
- `monitor_only.csv/jsonl`
- `stale_or_awarded_live_records.csv/jsonl`
- `english_insufficient_live_records.csv/jsonl`
- `rejected_near_misses.csv/jsonl`

Upper-funnel acquisition counters are not lead counts and must not be used as seller pipeline totals.
